# Miko Exet — Kid Storyboard & Image Briefs

Project: Miko Exet — STORY_TEC_V1
Format: Picture book / short illustrated story — each brief below is one printable page for the illustrator (A4 portrait, 300 DPI: 2480×3508 px recommended).

Notes for illustrator
- Tone: warm, slightly uncanny, playful. Think autumn fairs, soft lantern light, gentle creatures, feathered textures.
- Palette: Warm ochres (#DFA76B), soft teal (#7BC7C7), deep plum (#4B2E4F), neutral parchment (#F5EDE6).
- Characters: Miko (child, curious, short coat with oversized pockets), Exet (small companion creature — mothlike wings, glassy eyes), The Chronicle (tall, bookish figure, stitched map cloak).
- Typography/legibility: provide generous margins for type overlays; supply focal clear space for chapter headings.

Image briefs (one page = one illustration)

---

Page 1 — The Pocket Opening

Primary: Close three-quarter shot of Miko seated on a low step, reaching into an oversized coat pocket. Light from inside the pocket spills warm lunars; a sliver of luminous paper maps and a tiny glass lantern peek out.

Composition: Eye-level, slight low-angle to emphasize pocket scale. Miko centered-left to allow breathing space to the right for title overlays.

Color & Lighting: Warm pocket glow (soft yellow-orange) against cool evening teal background. Soft rim light on Miko's hair.

Key details: Visible stitched constellation on pocket edge, small wingtip of Exet peeking out, faint dust motes.

Mood/Notes: Wonder and invitation; the pocket is both intimate and portal-like.

---

Page 2 — Exet Revealed

Primary: Mid-shot of Exet emerging — mothlike wings folded, eyes like polished beads. It reaches a tiny hand toward Miko’s palm.

Composition: Two-thirds frame; Miko's silhouette blurred in foreground, Exet in focus.

Color & Lighting: Muted plum backdrop; warm pocket light illuminating Exet's translucent wings.

Key details: Texture on Exet's wings like old stained paper; tiny sewn button on Exet's collar.

Mood/Notes: First contact — mix of awe and caution.

---

Page 3 — Map That Moves

Primary: Overhead spread of an unfolding map on the ground; the map’s ink lines softly animate into miniature terrains — mountains rise like paper hills.

Composition: Bird's-eye; hands (Miko and Exet) pointing to a moving icon on the map.

Color & Lighting: Parchment neutrals, touches of plum for ink. Gentle directional light from top-left.

Key details: Tiny stitched compass sewn into map corner, faint script in an unknown hand.

Mood/Notes: Magic made tangible; hint that the map responds to intent.

---

Page 4 — Lantern Lane

Primary: Wide shot down a narrow lane lined with hanging lanterns; silhouettes of otherworldly shopfronts — a clock that runs backward, a window with a small sea in its sill.

Composition: Vanishing point down the lane; Miko walking with Exet perched on the shoulder.

Color & Lighting: Warm lantern oranges contrasted with deep teal shadows. Subtle fog.

Key details: Playful signage, small creatures in window displays, breadcrumb trail of luminous dust.

Mood/Notes: Curiosity and small wonder; this is the first glimpse of the world beyond the pocket.

---

Page 5 — The Chronicle’s Door

Primary: Front-facing door of the Chronicle’s bookshop — a tall arched door with a stained-glass map window. An inviting brass knocker shaped like a compass.

Composition: Symmetrical, Miko standing before it with a hand raised to knock.

Color & Lighting: Deep, cozy interiors visible through the map glass; exterior in twilight.

Key details: Poster advertising a “Midnight Cartography” event, a carved sign of an open eye.

Mood/Notes: Ceremony and threshold — this is a turning point.

---

Page 6 — Inside the Shop

Primary: Interior view — towering shelves of maps that flutter like pages, the Chronicle behind a counter made of stitched leather.

Composition: Low-angle to emphasize shelf height. Miko small in the foreground, eyes wide.

Color & Lighting: Amber lamplight, soft backlit floating dust.

Key details: Hanging maps labeled with odd place names, several maps animated, tiny ladder with a pulley.

Mood/Notes: Overwhelmed wonder; tactile books and maps.

---

Page 7 — A Map with a Memory

Primary: Close-up on a specific map that shows a child's memory — a house, a garden, a laughing face — that shifts into a star pattern.

Composition: Framed tightly on map details; a finger traces the memory path.

Color & Lighting: Soft golds and cool blues to indicate shifting states.

Key details: Faint impressions of handwriting, a tiny stitched heart icon that glows when touched.

Mood/Notes: Nostalgia and revelation; maps can hold memories.

---

Page 8 — Night Crossing

Primary: Miko and Exet crossing a bridge made of paper ribbons over a dark river of ink; reflections form tiny constellations.

Composition: Wide shot, diagonal bridge leading toward a distant lit town.

Color & Lighting: Inky blues, constellation reflections, soft rim glows.

Key details: Paper edges flutter, a small paperboat tied to Miko’s sleeve.

Mood/Notes: Quiet courage — the bridge is literal and metaphorical.

---

Page 9 — The Small Storm

Primary: Sudden gust lifts maps and lanterns; Miko clutches Exet close as flying maps whirl around.

Composition: Dynamic diagonal composition; motion emphasized by tilted lanterns.

Color & Lighting: High contrast between storm-swept lanterns and shadowed lanes.

Key details: One map wraps around Exet’s wing; Miko smiles despite the chaos.

Mood/Notes: Adventure’s teeth — excitement with a gentle threat.

---

Page 10 — Home Pocket

Primary: Warm, domestic shot: back on the step where the story began, Miko tucking a smaller map into a new pocket as dawn breaks.

Composition: Intimate close; Miko’s face lit by dawn, Exet asleep beside them.

Color & Lighting: Soft dawn palette; warm, reassuring light.

Key details: A new stitch added to the pocket (a mark of the journey), tiny map edge peeking out.

Mood/Notes: Return and subtle growth; ending with comfort and continued curiosity.
