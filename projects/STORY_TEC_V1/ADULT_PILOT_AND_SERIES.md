# Miko Exet — Adult Pilot & Series Bible (S1, 6 eps)

Series title (working): Miko Exet
Format: 6 x 30–36' episodes — serialized magical-realist drama with cozy, uncanny moments

Logline
Miko, a cartographer of small, private maps, partners with Exet — a tiny companion found in an enchanted pocket — to chart lost places of the heart, discovering that maps can hold memories, bargains, and the power to alter lives.

Core themes
- Memory as geography: places are stitched together by what we remember.
- Small bravery: growth comes from tiny choices and sustained curiosity.
- The ethics of navigation: maps give power — who gets to chart the world?

Season arc (S1)
Miko discovers the Chronicle’s bookshop and learns that maps can be bartered for memory. Over six episodes Miko must decide whether to fix a broken memory in exchange for the chance to map a lost home. By the finale Miko's choice reveals a larger cartographic network — the world is stitched together by the Chronicle and its strangers.

Tone & Style
Gentle, uncanny realism — grounded domestic moments punctuated by quiet, fantastical reveals. Cinematography: textured close-ups, warm-naturalistic light, hand-held intimacy.

Main characters
- Miko — protagonist: empathic, cautious, resourceful. Mid-20s (or ageless childlike adult), practical coat-wearer.
- Exet — companion: mothlike, shy, mischievous, reflective of Miko’s inner voice.
- The Chronicle — guardian/cartographer: enigmatic, keeps maps that store people’s pasts.
- Supporting cast: Ada (apprentice), Mr. Brine (shopkeeper rival), Townfolk with small magical trades.

Episode-by-episode beat sheets (S1 — 6 eps)

Episode 1 — "Pocket Maps"
Teaser: Miko lives a quiet life, finds Exet in an old coat pocket.
Act I: Miko learns Exet is tied to animated maps; they explore a familiar but shifting street.
Act II: The Chronicle’s shop appears; Miko trades a small memory for a map that leads to a childhood place.
Act III: The memory-map backfires — reveals a version of a home that cannot be returned to. Miko vows to learn how to control these maps.
Tag: Miko keeps the map; the Chronicle watches.

Episode 2 — "Ink and Echoes"
Teaser: A map reveals a lost friendship.
Act I: Miko tries to help a stranger using a memory-map; unintended consequences follow.
Act II: Exet’s own origins are hinted; a map resists being read.
Act III: Miko discovers rules: maps store, but they can be nested — maps inside maps.
Tag: A partial map glows in the Chronicle’s back room.

Episode 3 — "The Cartographer’s Trade"
Teaser: Market day; rival mapmakers hawk tidy, commodified memories.
Act I: Miko is tempted to sell a memory for coin.
Act II: Miko apprentices with Ada to learn preservation techniques.
Act III: A moral choice: buy someone's memory or help them keep it. Miko chooses the latter.
Tag: The Chronicle offers Miko a key to a restricted shelf.

Episode 4 — "Brine’s Ledger"
Teaser: Mr. Brine leverages maps for control.
Act I: A missing-person map leads Miko into a ledger of debts tied to memories.
Act II: Miko uncovers how maps can be forged and weaponized.
Act III: Miko sabotages a ledger, freeing a memory at personal cost.
Tag: Exet shows a new symbol etched into its wing.

Episode 5 — "Crossing the Fold"
Teaser: A map requires crossing a literal paper bridge.
Act I: Miko and Exet cross into a marginal place where maps flap like flags.
Act II: Confrontation with a map-guardian; Miko learns a secret history of the Chronicle.
Act III: The Chronicle reveals an old map of a town that Miko recognizes as home.
Tag: Miko finds a stitched heart — a memory that could restore someone’s past.

Episode 6 (Finale) — "Stitch and Return"
Teaser: Stakes rise as Mr. Brine closes in; the Chronicle is compromised.
Act I: Miko must choose what to mend with the stitched heart memory.
Act II: Confrontations culminate; tradeoffs are made.
Act III: Miko uses the memory-map to heal a small family history; chooses stewardship over profit.
Tag: The map network reveals more threads — larger world implied; Miko accepts a place in the Chronicle’s circle.

One-page series pitch (for buyers/packaging)
Miko Exet is an intimate magical-realist series about the small cartographies that make us who we are. Each episode explores a new map and the people bound to it; over a season, the series discovers a stitched-together world of memory-keepers and the costs of rearranging the past. Think: The Tenderness of Amélie mixed with the quiet ritual of The Curious Case of ... but anchored in hand-made maps and small moral choices.
